How the estimate is built

Every estimate should show its age, contributing signals, and confidence—not just a single number.

01

Measure approach delay

Compare traffic-aware travel time on a fixed approach segment with its normal travel time.

02

Verify with visitor timers

Use recent completed waits to calibrate the traffic signal and identify the actual back of the queue.

03

Account for disruptions

Flag roadwork, crashes, closures, weather, and gate-hour changes that can mimic or alter a normal entrance queue.

04

Forecast comparable days

Blend recent conditions with historical waits for the same entrance, season, weekday, hour, and weather pattern.

Potential production inputs
  • Traffic-routing API
  • Visitor wait timers
  • NPS alerts and entrance status
